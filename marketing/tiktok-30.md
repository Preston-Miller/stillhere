# Stillhere TikTok: 30 videos

Every question below is covered by the built-in library (CDC, FEMA, NWS, USFA, NPS, FDA, Army, NHS). Ask it in the app before you film to make sure the answer is good.

## The format (same every time)

1. **Hook, 0–2 s:** you on camera, or the Wi-Fi being turned off. The hook line goes on screen as text.
2. **Proof it's offline, 2–4 s:** click Wi-Fi off in the menu bar, or show the phone with no bars.
3. **Ask, 4–8 s:** type the question in Stillhere. Use my clip or record your own screen.
4. **Answer, 8–20 s:** show the answer, sped up, with the source chip ("CDC") in frame.
5. **Close, last 2 s:** "Stillhere. Mac app. Works with no internet. Link in bio," or say the URL out loud.

On-screen text everywhere, captions on, 15–30 s. Title format: **"No internet, day N: <question>"**.

## Other formats to mix in (about 1 in 4 videos)

- **Map, no signal:** phone with zero bars next to the laptop, zoom into your town, drop "Water" and "Shelter" pins. Hook: "Utah, here's your map if the towers go down."
- **Founder:** you on camera, 30 s. "I'm a BYU student. I built an AI that runs with no internet because… (your real reason)." Pin it to your profile.
- **Comment replies:** reply to comments asking questions ("does it work for X?") by asking Stillhere on screen. TikTok's reply-to-comment video feature is free reach.
- **News:** when a real storm or outage hits, post a calm "if you lose power tonight" video the same day. No fear-selling.

## Rules

- Post 1–2 a day for 30 days. Don't judge a single video; judge the format after about 10.
- Say "Mac app" in every video so iPhone/Android people don't feel tricked.
- Medical topics: let the cited answer do the talking and leave the "check the sources, especially for anything medical" line visible. Don't add your own medical claims.
- Track: Vercel Analytics → Referrers → tiktok.com, plus Stripe sales by day.
