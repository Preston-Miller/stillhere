Children's acetaminophen (up&up) 160 mg per 5 mL, dosing chart (FDA drug label)
Target Corporation Children’s Acetaminophen 160 mg / 5 mL Drug Facts

These directions apply to the specific product described below. Check the
actual product label before use; do not assume another brand has the same directions.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT (IN EACH 5 ML)
Acetaminophen 160 mg

DIRECTIONS
- this product does not contain directions or complete warnings for adult use
- do not give more than directed (see overdose warning)
- shake well before using
- mL = milliliter
- find right dose on chart below. If possible, use weight to dose; otherwise, use age.
- remove the child protective cap and squeeze your child’s dose into the dosing cup
- repeat dose every 4 hours while symptoms last
- do not give more than 5 times in 24 hours
- Children's acetaminophen 160 mg per 5 mL, child weighing under 24 lb (or, if the weight is not known, age under 2 years): ask a doctor.
- Children's acetaminophen 160 mg per 5 mL, child weighing 24 to 35 lb (or, if the weight is not known, age 2 to 3 years): give 5 mL.
- Children's acetaminophen 160 mg per 5 mL, child weighing 36 to 47 lb (or, if the weight is not known, age 4 to 5 years): give 7.5 mL.
- Children's acetaminophen 160 mg per 5 mL, child weighing 48 to 59 lb (or, if the weight is not known, age 6 to 8 years): give 10 mL.
- Children's acetaminophen 160 mg per 5 mL, child weighing 60 to 71 lb (or, if the weight is not known, age 9 to 10 years): give 12.5 mL.
- Children's acetaminophen 160 mg per 5 mL, child weighing 72 to 95 lb (or, if the weight is not known, age 11 years): give 15 mL.

*or as directed by a doctor
Attention: use only enclosed dosing cup specifically designed for use with this product. Do not use any other dosing device.

PURPOSE
Pain reliever/fever reducer

USES
temporarily:
- reduces fever
- relieves minor aches and pains due to:
- the common cold
- flu
- headache
- sore throat
- toothache

WARNINGS
Liver warning: This product contains acetaminophen. Severe liver damage may occur if your child takes
- more than 5 doses in 24 hours, which is the maximum daily amount
- with other drugs containing acetaminophen
Allergy alert: Acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions. Symptoms may include:
- skin reddening
- blisters
- rash
If a skin reaction occurs, stop use and seek medical help right away.
Sore throat warning: If sore throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, is accompanied or followed by fever, headache, rash, nausea, or vomiting, consult a doctor promptly.

DO NOT USE
- with any other drug containing acetaminophen (prescription or nonprescription). If you are not sure whether a drug contains acetaminophen, ask a doctor or pharmacist.
- if your child has ever had an allergic reaction to this product or any of its ingredients

ASK A DOCTOR BEFORE USE IF YOUR CHILD HAS
liver disease

ASK A DOCTOR OR PHARMACIST BEFORE USE IF YOUR CHILD IS
taking the blood thinning drug warfarin

WHEN USING THIS PRODUCT
do not exceed recommended dose (see overdose warning)

STOP USE AND ASK A DOCTOR IF
- pain gets worse or lasts more than 5 days
- fever gets worse or lasts more than 3 days
- new symptoms occur
- redness or swelling is present
These could be signs of a serious condition.

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.
Overdose warning: In case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away (1-800-222-1222). Quick medical attention is critical even if you do not notice any signs or symptoms.
