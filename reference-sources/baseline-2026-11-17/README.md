## Rights and attribution requirements

- **CDC:** agency-authored public-domain text only. Preserve substantive content; credit CDC, state free original availability, prominently disclaim endorsement. Image/media content excluded. [Policy](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html).
- **NHS England:** OGLv3 plus NHS-specific conditions permit commercial inclusion within the standard purchase/setup costs; no specific charge for NHS content. Text only. Each snapshot is dated with required attribution. Unchanged dated copies do not require perpetual online refresh; adaptations use the prescribed public-sector attribution and must not imply NHS clinical approval. Product must show a prominent licence statement. UK999/111 should not be silently mixed with U.S.911. [Terms](https://www.nhs.uk/our-policies/terms-and-conditions/) and [exclusions](https://www.nhs.uk/our-policies/terms-and-conditions/content-not-licensed-for-re-use/).
- **Army:** qualifying original U.S. government official-duty content is public domain in the U.S. under17USC105. Author/publisher/edition verified in each downloaded manual; text scans found no copyright/permission notices. This does not grant branding rights, establish foreign-law status or blanket-clear every third-party component. Text-first selection; images need a separate check. [Statute](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#105).
